COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE EPC & CONSTRUCTION AGREEMENT

BETWEEN: Apex Prime Properties Ltd ("Developer") and BuildMaster EPC Contractors ("Contractor")

SECTION 1: DELAY LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
1.1 Completion Milestone. Contractor guarantees Substantial Completion of the Commercial Tower within eighteen (18) months. For each calendar day of delay beyond the guaranteed date, Contractor shall pay Developer liquidated damages in the amount of Ten Thousand US Dollars ($10,000.00/day) as a direct offset against contract billings.

SECTION 2: INDEMNIFICATION & MECHANIC'S LIENS
2.1 Subcontractor Liens. Contractor shall unconditionally indemnify, protect, and defend Developer from any mechanic's liens, stop notices, or claims asserted by any subcontractor, supplier, or laborer, and shall bond around any such lien within five (5) business days at Contractor's sole expense.

SECTION 3: UNILATERAL TERMINATION
3.1 Developer Right to Terminate. Developer may terminate this Agreement and Contractor's employment hereunder at any time without cause upon seven (7) days written notice, taking possession of all Contractor materials, equipment, and permits on site without additional compensation.
