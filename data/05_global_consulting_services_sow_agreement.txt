GLOBAL CONSULTING SERVICES MASTER STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW)

BETWEEN: Enterprise Advisory Partners LLC ("Consultant") and Global Retail Conglomerate ("Client")

SECTION 1: EXTENDED PAYMENT TERMS
1.1 Payment Schedule. Client shall pay all undisputed invoiced fees within ninety (90) days following receipt of Consultant's invoice (Net 90 Days). Client shall have the unilateral right to withhold up to twenty-five percent (25%) of any payment as a performance holdback until final project signoff.

SECTION 2: PROPRIETARY METHODOLOGIES & TOOLS
2.1 Assignment of Consultant Frameworks. All analytical models, diagnostic frameworks, benchmark spreadsheets, and consulting methodologies utilized, modified, or prepared by Consultant shall become the exclusive intellectual property of Client upon delivery.

SECTION 3: EXTENDED RETROACTIVE AUDIT
3.1 Seven-Year Audit Access. Client or its authorized representatives shall have the right, at any time during the term and for seven (7) years following completion of the Services, to audit all of Consultant's internal books, emails, employee timesheets, billing records, and project files.
